How a score becomes a class

  • SAFE 0-10
    No meaningful indicators.
  • LOW RISK 11-30
    Minor signals; confirm it was expected.
  • SUSPICIOUS 31-60
    Several signals; verify before acting.
  • HIGH RISK 61-100
    Likely phishing; do not click, report it.
Thresholds are project assumptions to be calibrated on validation data.

If you clicked: incident playbook

  1. Disconnect from the network if you opened a file.
  2. Do not enter or re-use any credentials on the page.
  3. Change the password of any account you may have exposed, and enable MFA.
  4. Report the email and the time you clicked to your security team.
  5. Run a malware scan with your official security tool.
  6. Watch accounts for unusual sign-ins and write down what happened.
